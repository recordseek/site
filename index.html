What does RecordSeek do?

RecordSeek captures a web page as a source citation and helps you attach it to a person in your FamilySearch or Ancestry family tree. You can spend less time recording sources and more time researching.

Does it work with FamilySearch and Ancestry?

Yes. RecordSeek supports connecting web sources to both FamilySearch and Ancestry. You’ll use your account with the service you choose to attach a source to your family tree.

When can I get the mobile app?

The new RecordSeek mobile app is coming soon. The release date and download links will be shared when it is ready.

Can I use it without a browser extension?

Yes. The RecordSeek bookmarklet is an alternative for supported browsers and shared computers. Drag the button from the download section to your bookmarks bar, or follow the installation instructions linked above to get started.
